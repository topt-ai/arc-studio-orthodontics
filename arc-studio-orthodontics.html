The Practice
"Orthodontics is not just about straight teeth. It is about the confidence that follows."

Dr. Elena Vasquez founded Arc Studio in 2011 with a single conviction: that every patient deserves the time, care, and craft that most practices reserve for their most complex cases. With a background in both biomedical engineering and clinical orthodontics, she brings a rare combination of technical precision and artistic sensibility to every treatment plan.

How It Works

Your path to
a new smile.

A clear, four-step journey from consultation to the final reveal.

01

Free Consultation

Meet Dr. Vasquez for a no-pressure conversation about your smile goals. Includes digital X-rays and a full clinical assessment.

02

3D Smile Mapping

We take a precise digital scan of your teeth and model your projected outcome so you can see the final result before committing.

03

Custom Treatment

Your treatment plan is built around your anatomy, timeline, and budget. Progress visits every 6 to 8 weeks with direct access to our team.

04

The Reveal

Braces off, retainers fitted, and photos taken. Your smile is now yours to keep, and we are here for the long term to make sure it stays that way.
